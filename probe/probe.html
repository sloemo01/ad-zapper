How to run the drill
  1. Reload the target tab before attaching. That load is your baseline; it shows up under load timing.
  2. Pick the tab above, tick the boxes you want, hit Attach & start. The tab gets a "started debugging this browser" banner. DevTools can no longer attach to it while the probe is running.
  3. Reload the target tab, then scroll it for twenty or thirty seconds. The reload after attach is the number that matters.
  4. With rewrite on, the target tab's title becomes CDP-REWRITE-OK, which is the proof that the response body was edited before the page saw it.
  5. Hit Stop & detach and compare p95 handling time plus the attached load time against the baseline. Under about 5 ms p95 and loads within roughly 20% of baseline means this door is usable.
